Print Shop Manager

The in-house app of Cyprus Graphics, Limassol

Print Shop Manager is the desktop app we use in our print shop to make quotes, keep the job book, look up our price list and keep track of paper, materials and machines. It runs on the shop's own computers (Mac and Windows) and is used only by Cyprus Graphics staff. It is not sold or offered to the public.

Google Drive

The app can sign in with a Google account to keep the shop's data the same on all our computers. It saves its data files in that account's Google Drive and reads them back from there.

  • It asks for one Google permission only: “See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app.”
  • So it can open only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.
  • The app has no server of its own. The data goes only between the shop's computers and Google Drive, and it is never sold or shared.
