Privacy Policy

Print Shop Manager · Last updated 8 November 2026

Print Shop Manager (“the app”) is the in-house desktop app of Cyprus Graphics (Evros Neofytides), Ipeirou 11, 3040 Limassol, Cyprus. It is used only by Cyprus Graphics staff on the shop's own computers. This page explains what the app does with data from a Google account.

What the app uses from Google

When you sign in with Google in the app, it asks for one permission: to see, edit, create and delete only the Google Drive files you use with the app. With it, the app:

  • creates a folder with the shop's data files in your Google Drive, and reads, updates and deletes those files to keep the shop's computers in sync;
  • reads the name and email address of the signed-in account, to show which account it is using.

The app cannot see any other file in your Google Drive, and it does not use Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

Where the data is kept

  • In your Google Drive: the shop's data files the app created.
  • On the shop's computers: a copy of the shop's data, and the sign-in key Google gives the app, stored encrypted.

The app has no server of its own. Nothing is sent anywhere other than your own Google Drive.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Print Shop Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Sign out of Google in the app (Settings › Data, sync & backup) to delete its sign-in key from that computer.
  • Remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account, at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • The app's files stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
